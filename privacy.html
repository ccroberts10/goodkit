Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

GoodKit ("we," "our," or "us") operates the peer-to-peer cycling gear marketplace at good-kit.com. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use and protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you list an item: your email address, item details, photos, and requested price.

When you buy an item: your name, email address, and shipping address. Payment card details are processed directly by Stripe — we never see or store your card number.

When you create a shop account: your business name, contact email, and shop description.

Automatically: standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps). We do not use tracking pixels or third-party behavioral advertising cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To complete transactions between buyers and sellers
  • To generate shipping labels and tracking
  • To send transactional emails (listing confirmation, sale notification, shipping updates)
  • To detect and prevent fraud
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

3. Information We Share

We share limited information only as needed to complete transactions:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data.
  • Shippo / USPS / UPS / FedEx — shipping label generation and carrier APIs receive the recipient's name and address.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery.
  • Amazon S3 / Cloudflare R2 — photo storage.

When a sale completes, the buyer's shipping address is shared with the seller solely to fulfill the shipment. Sellers agree not to use buyer contact information for any other purpose.

4. Data Retention

We retain transaction records for 7 years for tax and legal compliance. Listing data (photos, descriptions) is retained while the listing is active and for 90 days after deletion. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting us at the address below.

5. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are not stored — we use magic-link email authentication. Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe (PCI-DSS compliant). We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, though no system is completely secure.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Colorado residents have additional rights under the Colorado Privacy Act (CPA). California residents have rights under CCPA. To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address below.

7. Children

GoodKit is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. Material changes will be communicated via email to registered users. Continued use of GoodKit after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
