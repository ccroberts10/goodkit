About GoodKit

Built by riders.
For riders.

GoodKit is a peer-to-peer cycling gear marketplace. No auction nonsense, no 47 seller tiers, no $7.99 "featured listing" upsells. You list your gear, someone buys it, you keep 85%. We handle the rest — and we give back to the trails while we're at it.

Giving back
$—
donated to trail maintenance so far

Every sale funds the trails you ride.

We give 10% of GoodKit's fee from every completed sale to the International Mountain Bicycling Association (IMBA) — the organization that fights for trail access and funds the trail crews doing the actual work.

It's not a marketing stunt. It's not opt-in. It happens automatically, every sale, no strings. Because if we're going to build a business out of people buying and selling the gear they ride, the least we can do is make sure there are trails to ride it on.

🌲 Official IMBA Trail Partner

IMBA operates in all 50 states and 40+ countries. They've built or improved over 90,000 miles of trails. When you buy or sell on GoodKit, you're part of that.

How it works

Simple by design.

1

List your gear in minutes

Photos, price, condition. We detect the brand, suggest keywords, and handle all the SEO so your listing gets found on Google — not just inside our app.

2

Buyer pays securely

Stripe handles payment. No awkward Venmo requests, no "can you mark as gift?" DMs. Buyers are protected for 72 hours after delivery.

3

Ship with discounted labels

We generate a prepaid Shippo label at our negotiated rate — cheaper than the post office. Drop it at USPS or UPS, done.

4

Get paid directly

Funds go straight to your bank via Stripe Connect. No waiting for GoodKit to cut you a check. We never hold your money.

Why we exist

Cycling gear deserves a better home.

Facebook Marketplace means blurry photos, lowball offers, and meeting strangers in parking lots. eBay means competing against businesses selling new gear at a discount. Pinkbike is great if you're technical enough to navigate it and comfortable with zero protections.

GoodKit is for everyone in between — the rider who upgraded their fork and needs to move the old one, the person getting into gravel and looking for a deal on a kit, the cyclist who bought a saddle that just doesn't work for them. Real people, real gear, real prices.
