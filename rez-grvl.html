What happened

They built something real. Then got a cease-and-desist.

Rez GRVL is an Indigenous-led adventure ride on the Gila River Indian Reservation in Sacaton, Arizona — January 9–10, 2027. It raises money for nonprofits and brings riders to land that cycling rarely touches.

SBT GRVL® holds a federal trademark on "GRVL" for cycling events. They contacted the organizer — publicly, on Instagram — asking her to rename the event and rebrand. Fully within their legal rights. Also a gut punch to a grassroots community ride.

The letter, in short "We own the federally registered GRVL® trademark for cycling events… we're asking that you change the event name to REZ Gravel, or another variation that doesn't use GRVL®, and update the associated branding." — Ryan Steers, VP GRVL Events, SBT GRVL
